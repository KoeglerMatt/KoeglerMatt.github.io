A finalized statement that stays final.

An eight-month billing rewrite, from workflow definition through validation.

Completed project
Define the issueMap the workflowDevelop & testValidate the record

Why it mattered

Customer statements needed to be dependable records of completed billing periods. Finance, customer-facing teams, and customers all relied on the same information.

The problem

Later account activity could change a finalized statement. That made it harder to explain a billing period and rely on the statement as a fixed record.

My role

I owned the end-to-end business workflow and development of the associated reporting. I partnered with Finance, Product, and Engineering on requirements, implementation, testing, and rollout; engineering delivery was a cross-functional effort.

The approach

  1. Documented the existing statement behavior and the business requirements for a finalized record.
  2. Connected the billing workflow and reporting requirements across Finance, Product, and Engineering.
  3. Worked through testing and validation to check the revised behavior against the original problem.
  4. Carried the business workflow through rollout and reporting delivery.
What changed
  • Delivered the billing rewrite over eight months.
  • Finalized statements remained fixed after finalization.
  • Customers and internal teams had a dependable record of the completed billing period.

What I built

A revised billing workflow and supporting reporting that preserved finalized statements after subsequent account activity.

What I’d do next

Extend regression coverage to statement edge cases and track recurring billing exceptions so new changes preserve the same control.
