Why it mattered

A commission process needs to translate approved compensation plans into consistent calculations that Finance and Sales can understand and review.

The problem

The manual process was becoming harder to maintain. Moving into a platform required translating plan rules carefully, validating the underlying data, and investigating unexpected outputs.

My role

I contributed to the CaptivateIQ implementation by translating compensation requirements, validating source data and outputs, and investigating exceptions. My contribution sat within the broader implementation effort.

The approach

  1. Worked from approved compensation requirements to define calculation logic.
  2. Checked source data and calculated outputs for consistency with those requirements.
  3. Investigated exceptions during testing and supported the implementation work.
What changed
  • Translated compensation requirements into system calculation logic.
  • Validated source data and outputs and investigated exceptions during implementation.
  • Supported the move from manual calculations to a structured commission platform.

What I built

Calculation logic and validation work supporting the move from a manual commission process into CaptivateIQ.
