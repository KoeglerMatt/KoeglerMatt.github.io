How I think

Curious. Intentional.
Organized.

A reliable operation needs more than a correct spreadsheet. It needs clear ownership, consistent inputs, and a process people can follow.

I started in client services, helping merchants and partners understand their accounts. Moving into finance and operations gave me a view of the systems behind those questions: billing, payments, partner compensation, reconciliation, and reporting.

That experience shapes where I start. I learn how the work moves, who depends on it, and where the handoffs break. Then I connect the source data, define the rules, reconcile the differences, and build reporting around the decisions people need to make.

I’m used to presenting the same information several ways for Sales, Finance, partners, and leadership. The numbers should stay consistent; the explanation should fit the audience. I’ve also managed and trained direct reports, documented recurring work, and partnered across teams to deliver system changes.

My aim is a process the business can keep using: understandable, maintainable, and ready for improvement as the needs change.
